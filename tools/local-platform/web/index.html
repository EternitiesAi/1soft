    Recovery / Retained history

    Keep the original decisions.

    Download an encrypted, recovery-only record of the shared wallet and delivery journal, even after current evidence expires. It retains authenticated history, signed originals, attempts, responses, receipts and unresolved obligations.

    This includes other roles and hidden generic decisions. It excludes private keys, vaults, private invoice content, invoice/request/separate receipt books and any replaceable read caches. The alpha format includes the complete retained authenticated block and checkpoint history once. It is not a complete account backup or an active account restore, and does not renew spending authority, prove latestness or fence an old device.

    Keep the file, its separate backup passphrase and original genesis/policy pins privately. A lost passphrase cannot be recovered. Legacy workspaces allow 8 MiB of original history and a 16 MiB encrypted file. The explicitly selected durable alpha allows 64 MiB of authenticated history and a 180 MiB encrypted file. An over-capacity export is refused without dropping records.

    Use 16–1024 UTF-8 bytes. This passphrase encrypts history; it is separate from your development vault password.

    Keep the boundary visible.

    Legacy RPC proof collection stops at block 64. Fresh durable-alpha workspaces retain up to 4,096 blocks in authenticated batches of at most 64, with a fixed 24-hour horizon and an uninterrupted sequence of one-hour checkpoint trust windows. An expired gap cannot be renewed. Pause the local network with Ctrl+C when finished. Original decisions remain recoverable. Portable capsules include full related chain history and retain the original deadline.
